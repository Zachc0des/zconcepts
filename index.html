The Journey

Six years of following the markets — breaks included.

I have been following my passion for market participation for an estimated total of over six years, with breaks in between — breaks which are needed.

Stepping away isn't quitting. Rest resets perspective, protects capital, and protects you. The gaps in this journey are part of the process, not interruptions to it.

The Perspective

You don't have to rush in. You don't have to be in at all.

Most market content pushes urgency — act now, don't miss out, get in early. This is the opposite. The goal here is to slow the process down: observe first, simulate second, and only then consider whether real participation in financial instruments fits your life, your finances, and your temperament.

Participation is potential, not required. Watching price action, running back tests, and studying structure are complete activities on their own. If you ever do participate, it should be with money you can afford to lose, an amount that lets you sleep, and a plan for taking breaks.
